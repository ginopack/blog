# Hey, I'm Gino 👋

For the past 15+ years, I've been knee-deep in ERP systems and technology transformation — helping companies untangle complicated operational challenges and turn them into strategies that actually work. My focus today centers on **Business Central**, the broader **Microsoft 365 ecosystem**, and how **AI** is reshaping what's possible across both.

I started this blog to write down what I keep learning along the way — from real Business Central implementations to how AI tools are (and aren't) changing the day-to-day of ERP and business operations.

Some of what you can expect:

- **Business Central deep dives** — configuration, extensions, migrations, and lessons from the field
- **AI in the Microsoft stack** — Copilot, AI Builder, and where automation genuinely moves the needle versus where it's still hype
- **Microsoft 365 integration** — connecting Business Central with Power Platform, Teams, and the rest of the ecosystem
- Occasional recaps from conferences and user groups I've spoken at

Curious about my background? Head over to [About](/blog/about/). If you're looking for talk recordings or slides, check out [Speaking Engagements](/blog/speaking/).
